Privacy Policy

How Formatoff handles your files, purchases and information.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Formatoff is a file conversion app for iOS and Android for images, videos and audio, with additional PDF and ZIP tools. This policy covers the iOS and Android apps and these support pages. Platform-specific services are identified below. No account registration is required.

Your files are converted on your device. Formatoff does not upload selected files to a developer-operated conversion server. App Store or Google Play purchases and Google advertising or consent services involve separate network connections and data processing.

1. File processing

Files you choose through your device’s photo picker, file picker or supported sharing options are read for conversion on your device. Formatoff creates working copies and new output files; your original files remain unchanged. Files, filenames and conversion contents are not intentionally sent to advertising services.

Image conversion can remove metadata, including EXIF and GPS information, when the relevant removal option is enabled. Do not assume every output is free of metadata; review your settings before sharing.

Choosing a file stored in iCloud, Google Drive or another file provider may require that provider to download it. Saving or sharing an output through a cloud service or another app is your choice and is governed by that service’s policies. These transfers are separate from Formatoff’s conversion processing.

2. Local storage and deletion

Formatoff stores conversion history locally, including filenames, formats, file sizes, dates and links to outputs. Converted files and some previews remain in the app’s storage so you can reopen, save or share them. Preferences such as appearance, language and conversion settings are also stored locally.

A local usage ledger records daily task counts, task status and rewarded conversion credits to apply free usage limits and prevent duplicate rewards. Clearing conversion history does not reset this ledger or erase Apple’s or Google Play’s purchase records.

Temporary working files and export copies are normally removed after their operation finishes. A failed or interrupted task can leave temporary files until subsequent cleanup. Use Settings → Storage → Clear temporary files to remove eligible temporary data; files needed by active selections may be retained.

Delete individual results or use the history clearing option to remove local conversion records and their app-managed outputs. This does not delete your originals or copies already saved to your photo library, shared storage or other apps. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to your operating system’s backup, restore and data-retention behavior. Device backups, iCloud backups or Android backup services may contain app data according to your device and account settings.

3. Lifetime Pro and app store payments

Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. On iOS, Formatoff uses Apple’s StoreKit 2 to load the price, make purchases, verify signed transactions and restore Pro access.

On Android, Google Play Billing handles Pro purchases and provides purchase status and transaction information, such as a purchase token, so the app can determine Pro access. Google processes the payment and maintains its billing records. Formatoff does not receive your full payment card number or Google Account password. Purchases and restoration are managed through the platform and store account used.

On iOS, Apple processes your payment and manages its billing records. Formatoff does not receive your full payment card number or Apple Account password. The app uses purchase and entitlement information provided by StoreKit to determine whether Pro is active. There is no separate developer-operated purchase verification server in the iOS app.

Loading prices, purchasing and restoring can require internet access. Apple’s handling of purchase information is described in App Store & Privacy. Google’s handling of Google Play purchase information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Optional rewarded ads and consent

The free version integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose a rewarded ad to earn an additional conversion credit. Pro does not show rewarded ads. Consent information may be requested when the free app starts, even if you do not watch an ad.

The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean that no data is collected. Depending on the service, device permissions and configuration, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app identifiers, device and operating system information, advertising impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Where required, a consent form is presented before ads are requested. Use Settings → Ad privacy choices when available to review or change applicable choices. On iOS, Formatoff does not request App Tracking Transparency permission to access the advertising identifier. On Android, advertising identifiers may be processed depending on SDK configuration and device settings. You can manage available advertising ID controls in your Android device settings; the options and their location vary by Android version. This does not eliminate other SDK data processing.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosures for iOS and Android. Google controls its own retention practices; changing a consent choice does not necessarily delete information already processed.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

Formatoff does not include a separate Firebase Analytics, Sentry or Crashlytics integration, or a developer-operated analytics endpoint. Google’s advertising SDK can nevertheless process analytics, crash and performance data as described above. Apple or Google may also collect diagnostics and make reports available to developers according to your device’s analytics and privacy settings.

6. Permissions, notifications and Live Activities

Photo, media and file access is used for items you select and for saving outputs when you allow it. On iOS, you can manage Photos access in Settings. On Android, access may be granted through the system photo or file picker, or through media permissions where required by the Android version and feature. You can review available permissions in your device’s app settings. Denying access may prevent the corresponding import or save action.

Conversion completion notifications are local notifications; Formatoff does not use a developer remote-push server for them. Progress notifications on supported platforms and iOS Live Activities may display a filename, conversion status or remaining file count on your lock screen, notification panel or, on supported iPhones, Dynamic Island. Other people with access to your screen may see that information. You can manage notifications and previews in your device settings, and Live Activities in iOS Settings. Android may request notification permission where required by the operating system.

7. Support messages and this website

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to investigate and respond to your request and is retained as needed to handle support or meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending confidential files or payment credentials.

These static pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts or app-managed cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. Email and other external service providers handle information under their own policies and may process it in other countries.

8. Security and retention

Formatoff uses the operating system’s app storage environment and system permission controls. Local files remain until removed through app controls, cleanup or app removal, subject to backup behavior. The app does not provide an additional encrypted vault or promise secure erasure. No device, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Keep your device and app updated, protect your device with a passcode and review outputs before sharing. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete conversion files stored only on your device.

9. Children’s privacy

Formatoff is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact support so the issue can be reviewed and appropriate action taken. Parents and guardians can use Apple’s Screen Time and purchase restrictions, or Google Family Link and Google Play parental and purchase controls, as available.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can delete local history and outputs, clear temporary files, change device permissions, review available ad privacy choices and stop using the app. Copies exported to another service must be managed through that service.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant privacy authority. Contact the developer for requests concerning information the developer actually holds, such as support correspondence. For information controlled by Apple or Google, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. Reasonable verification may be needed to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, services or privacy practices change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised last-updated date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this page as appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Formatoff app developer at formatoff.support@gmail.com.
