Formatoff converts images, videos and audio on your iPhone, with additional tools for PDFs and ZIP archives. Your originals stay unchanged, and you choose where to save the results.

Common questions

How do I convert a file?

Tap the centre + button or choose a converter from Home. Select a file from Photos or Files, review the selection and continue. Choose the output format and available quality, compression or resize settings, then tap Convert. When it finishes, preview the result and save or share it.

Which formats are supported?

Images: HEIC/HEIF, JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and BMP inputs, with JPG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and BMP output options where available on your device.

Video: supported MOV, M4V and MP4 inputs can be converted to MP4. You can also extract audio as MP3 or M4A.

Audio: supported MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, AIFF, CAF and FLAC inputs can be converted to MP3, M4A or WAV, subject to iOS decoding support.

PDF merge, split and compression, plus ZIP creation and extraction, are available through Tools. A filename extension alone does not guarantee compatibility: the codec, encryption, file structure and device capabilities also matter. The app’s format selector shows available output choices.

How does batch conversion work?

With Pro, select multiple supported images, videos or audio files in the same converter, review the queue and apply the conversion settings. Batches support up to 20 files. Progress shows the current file and queue position. Review each result, because one unsupported or damaged file may fail while other files succeed.

PDF merging and ZIP creation combine selected inputs into one task and are separate from media batch conversion.

Where are converted files saved?

Outputs are initially stored inside Formatoff and appear in History. Use Save to Photos for supported media, Save to Files to choose a folder, or Share to send a copy to another app.

Saving requires the appropriate permission and enough free space. Deleting an app-managed result or clearing history removes its local output; copies you already exported and your original files stay where you saved them. Export important results before removing the app.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that purchased Pro, connect to the internet, open Settings or the Pro page and tap Restore purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

If access does not return, confirm the purchase appears in your Apple purchase history, check for pending approval or a refund, and try again. If purchase options cannot load, use Retry and check your connection. Contact support if the problem continues; never send your Apple Account password or full payment details.

What happens if a conversion fails?

The original file stays unchanged. Read the error message, check the file is fully available on your device, and try a supported output format or a smaller file. Failed or cancelled tasks do not use a successful free conversion. If other files in a batch finish successfully, those results remain available.

Temporary working files can remain after an interruption. Clear temporary files from Settings when no conversion is running, then retry.

How do the free allowance and rewarded ads work?

The free allowance is five successful tasks per day, one at a time. It resets at local midnight. PDF merge, split and ZIP operations each count as one task. Pro removes the daily limit and enables media batches.

When offered, completing a rewarded ad earns one additional conversion credit for that day. Closing an ad before its reward is earned does not grant a credit. Ads need internet access and may not always be available. Pro does not show rewarded ads.

Will compression always make a file smaller?

No. Some source formats are already highly compressed. Output size depends on the format, dimensions, bitrate and quality you choose. Increasing quality cannot restore detail already missing from the original.

PDF compression can rasterize pages and may remove searchable text, links, forms or vector detail. Keep your original and inspect the result before sharing it.

Try these steps first

  1. Check the source. Open it in Photos or Files and make sure any cloud download has finished. Password-protected or damaged files may not be supported.
  2. Free up storage. A conversion can need space for the input copy, temporary data and output. Clear temporary files in Settings or remove unneeded results.
  3. Try a smaller task. Reduce dimensions or quality, or convert one file to isolate a problem. Large or complex files can exceed device or tool limits.
  4. Keep the app open for long jobs. iOS may pause work in the background. A Live Activity does not guarantee unlimited background processing.
  5. Review permissions. Check Formatoff’s Photos and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Notifications are optional; enabling them allows completion alerts.
  6. Update and retry. Install the latest available app and iOS updates. If a problem persists, reopen the app and retry after preserving any important outputs.
